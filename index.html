Not one number. A spectrum.

Spectriq is a set of puzzles without words — patterns, 3D blocks, sequences to remember, symbols to spot against the clock. At the end you see how you did in four areas, compared with people your age, instead of a single score.

Four areas, two puzzles each

What the result looks like

A profile first

The main screen shows the four areas side by side, which ones stand out, and a percentile — “better than 78% of people your age”. That says more than any single number, and it is easier to read honestly.

Age matters: memory and speed peak at different ages than reasoning does, so you are always compared with your own age group.

An IQ figure, in small print

If you want it, there is an IQ-style figure too — shown as a range, like 108–118, never as one exact number, because a test on a phone can't be that precise.

The app measures each answer to the millisecond and checks your phone's touch delay at the start, so a slow screen doesn't count against you.

Where the numbers come from

To say “better than 78% of people your age”, the app needs to know how people of each age usually do. We build those comparison tables — the norms — from open scientific sources.

Published studies

Peer-reviewed research

Open-access papers that report average scores by age for memory, speed and reasoning tasks like ours — sequences of digits, light-up squares, symbol coding, pattern grids, rotation.

Public datasets

Open survey data

Large public-domain datasets such as the U.S. CDC's NHANES health survey, which includes a timed symbol-coding task, recalculated by us.

Population statistics

Who lives where

Age, sex and education figures from the UN, the World Bank, Eurostat and national censuses, used to balance the sample so it looks like the real population.

We use the published figures, never the questions of other tests: every puzzle in Spectriq is our own, generated from rules. Over time, people who choose to share their results anonymously help adjust the norms to the phone format. Each result shows which version of the norms it was calculated with.

For entertainment, honestly

Spectriq is built on published science, but it is a game, not an instrument. It has not been clinically validated, and it is not a medical, psychological, educational or professional assessment of any kind.

Your result can move with sleep, mood, a noisy room or a small screen. Treat it as a fun snapshot — not as a diagnosis, and not as a reason to make decisions about yourself, your child, your studies or anyone's job. If you have real concerns about memory, attention or development, talk to a qualified specialist.

Private by default

  • No sign-up, no email, no name. No ads and no analytics in this version.
  • The test runs entirely on the phone and works without the internet. Your history stays on your device.
  • Sharing results anonymously to improve the norms is optional and off until you turn it on — and for a child, only a parent can turn it on.
  • Even then: no name, no contacts, no location, no advertising ID. The Privacy Policy lists every field.
