What the app is

Spectriq is a collection of puzzles — patterns, 3D blocks, memory sequences and speed tasks — with a playful summary of how you did compared with other people your age. The puzzles are generated by the app; the comparison tables are put together from figures published in open scientific sources and, over time, from results that users choose to share anonymously.
